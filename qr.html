📱 EXPO GO MOBILE RUNNER

Run ResQLink on Android

Experience the full offline disaster management platform on your Android phone in 3 easy steps without compiling code.

1

Install Expo Go on Android

Download the free Expo Go app from the Google Play Store on your phone.

2

Start the Project Server

In your terminal inside the project root folder, run:

npx expo start --tunnel
3

Scan the Terminal QR Code

Open Expo Go on your phone, tap "Scan QR code", and scan the QR code printed in your computer terminal. The app will immediately bundle and run natively!
